## Release summary

This is an update to the previously released `pubformat` package.

Version 0.2.0 adds publication-ready formatting for t and F statistics and several commonly reported effect sizes, including Cohen's d, Hedges' g, eta squared, partial eta squared, and omega squared.

The release also expands support for confidence intervals and styled statistical symbols while preserving the package's separation of statistical calculation, interpretation, and publication formatting.
